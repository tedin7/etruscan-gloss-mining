## B

**baca** – "berry" (Latin term of possible Tyrrhenian origin)

**balma** – "cave" (Alpine Romance, possibly from Rhaetian)

**balteus** – "sword belt" (Romans classified this as Etruscan)

**barga** – "hut" (Alpine Romance, possibly from Rhaetian)

**baró, barónis** – "strong man, lout" (Latin derived from Etruscan)

**bassus** – "low" (Latin, possibly of Etruscan origin)

**bat-** – "yawning" (Latin root, possibly Etruscan; evolved into Old French forms meaning "to yawn" or "to bay")

**battuere** – "to beat" (Latin, possibly of Etruscan origin)

**bonus** – "good" (possibly of Etruscan origin)

**brenti** – "heather" (Tuscan term of Etruscan origin)

**buttis** – "barrel" (possibly of Tyrrhenian origin)

## D

**Dana** - Latinized name

**dorsum** - "the back" (Latin, possibly of Etruscan origin)

**duellum** - Evolved into the Latin term **bellum**, meaning "war". This word connects to Bellona, the warfare goddess depicted carrying a crimson whip and traveling by chariot, suggesting Etruscan roots
